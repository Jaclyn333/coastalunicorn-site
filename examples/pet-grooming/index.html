Welcome

A private studio-salon for your pet

Salty Paws is a private, appointment-only grooming studio in Panama City Beach. We keep things quiet and unhurried — rarely more than a pup or two in the studio at once — so every dog gets calm, focused, one-on-one attention.

We care for all sizes and breeds and specialize in finding the perfect "style" for your pet. Most visits take under two hours. Come see why local dogs (and their humans) love us.
