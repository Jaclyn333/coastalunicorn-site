Teaching

Maya offers private lessons in drums & percussion, composition, and music theory — grounded in curiosity, fundamentals, and sound. All levels welcome, from first-time beginners to working musicians.

  • Drums & Percussion — technique, time, and vocabulary
  • Composition — writing for small ensembles and beyond
  • Music Theory — ear training and harmony, made approachable
  • In-person in Brooklyn or online worldwide
